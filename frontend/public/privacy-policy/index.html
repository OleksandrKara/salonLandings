This Privacy Policy explains what information AK.LUX.NAILS ("we," "us") collects when you book an appointment, visit our website, or communicate with us by text message or email, and how we use, share, and protect that information.

Information we collect

  • Booking information — your name, phone number, email address, and the appointment details you provide when scheduling a visit through our online booking widget (powered by Square).
  • Communications — the content of text messages and emails you exchange with us, including appointment confirmations, reminders, and any replies you send us.
  • Payment information — payments are processed directly by Square; we do not store your full card number on our own systems.
  • Website usage data — general analytics and advertising data (e.g. via the Meta Pixel) about how visitors use our site, collected through cookies and similar technologies. See "Cookies & advertising" below.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and manage your appointments.
  • To send appointment confirmations and reminders by text message and/or email.
  • To send marketing text messages and emails (special offers, rebooking reminders) — but only if you have opted in to receive them. See "Text messaging" below.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To improve our services and our website.
  • To measure and improve the effectiveness of our advertising.

Text messaging

If you provide your phone number when booking or opt in through our website, we may send you appointment-related text messages (confirmations, reminders, and satisfaction check-ins) and, if you've separately agreed to marketing messages, promotional offers.

  • Consent to receive marketing text messages is never a condition of booking or purchase.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages from us.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How we share information

We work with a small number of service providers to run our business, and we share information with them only as needed to provide our services:

  • Square — for appointment booking, point-of-sale, and payment processing.
  • Twilio — to deliver our text messages.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — for advertising analytics via the Meta Pixel.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our clients.

Cookies & advertising

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors use our site and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site functionality.

Data retention

We keep booking and communication records for as long as reasonably necessary for business, tax, and legal purposes, or until you ask us to delete information we're not otherwise required to keep.

Your choices

  • Opt out of marketing text messages anytime by replying STOP.
  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us below.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
